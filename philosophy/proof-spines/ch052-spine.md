## Claim
\textbf{Distinction holonomy} occurs when loop transport changes a claim, \(T_\gamma(J)\neq J\), revealing path-dependent drift even where formal continuity is preserved.

## Load-bearing steps
1. Building on transport \(T_\gamma\), the chapter studies loops through police, media, court, archive, and public memory rather than one-way procedural motion.
2. When a claim \(J\) returns from such a loop with \(T_\gamma(J)\neq J\), the institution exhibits distinction holonomy.
3. The point is not gross contradiction but path residue: each local transmission may look defensible while the full circuit changes the meaning or force of the distinction.
4. This explains how repeated representation can transform an accusation without any single transmission openly declaring a new category.
5. The load-bearing conclusion is that formal continuity tests are insufficient unless institutions also inspect how circulation itself alters what their distinctions now do.
