## Claim
Proof is the authorized evidentiary path $E \xrightarrow{\operatorname{admit}} E^\ast \xrightarrow{\operatorname{test}} \widetilde E \xrightarrow{\operatorname{weigh}} J \xrightarrow{\operatorname{authorize}} A$: legal judgment differs from factual belief because coercion is licensed only by moving through those public transformations.

## Depends on
- ch034 (Privacy and Future Reinterpretation) — because facts and traces can always be redescribed later and renewed suspicion can persist indefinitely, coercive action needs a public pathway that disciplines when reinterpretation may justify judgment.

## Load-bearing steps
1. Raw material $E$ may ground suspicion, but it is not yet legally usable judgment; the chapter opens by separating factual belief from institutionally warranted action.
2. The admission stage $E \xrightarrow{\operatorname{admit}} E^\ast$ assigns material a procedural role inside the record, turning free-floating information into evidence with a public status.
3. The testing stage $E^\ast \xrightarrow{\operatorname{test}} \widetilde E$ matters because admitted material must survive contradiction, foundation, and adversarial pressure before it can carry institutional weight.
4. Only after the weighing stage $\widetilde E \xrightarrow{\operatorname{weigh}} J$ does the tribunal form judgment, and only the authorization stage $J \xrightarrow{\operatorname{authorize}} A$ licenses coercive action.
5. Because each arrow is a distinct operator and a possible refusal point, proof is an architecture for halting unsupported escalation from appearance to action, not a psychology of inward certainty.

## External cases / technical analogues

| Case | Role | Note |
|---|---|---|
| The staged sequence of charge, answer, proof, and verdict in a criminal trial | Illustrative | Shows concretely that law does not treat accusation, evidence, and judgment as interchangeable moments, but assigns each a different procedural role before coercion becomes final. |

## Objections this chapter must survive
- **Objection:** If the underlying aim is truth, then an elaborate proof architecture can look like fetishized procedure: when decision-makers already have strong reason to think someone is guilty or liable, formal sequencing merely delays justice.
- **Response:** Under coercive stakes, disciplined sequencing is what distinguishes warranted judgment from intensified suspicion; without it, confidence in appearances simply becomes a self-ratifying permission structure.
