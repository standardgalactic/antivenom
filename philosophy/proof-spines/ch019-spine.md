# Spine: ch019 — Hierarchies of Salience

**Volume:** II: Causal Explosion
**Part:** IV. Time, Delay, and the Maturation of Evidence
**Status:** spine

## Claim
Hierarchies of salience are governed by reach probabilities \(P_{\mathrm{reach}}=\prod_{i=0}^{L-1}p_i\) over levels \(E_0\to\cdots\to E_L\), so evidence can be public yet still practically invisible to decision-makers.

## Depends on
- ch017 — the force of evidence depends on its temporal position within an inquiry.
- ch018 — long accumulations of observation time can be highly probative even when they are narratively inert.

## Load-bearing steps
1. Because evidence often passes through levels \(E_0\to E_1\to\cdots\to E_L\), its practical force depends on transmission through a hierarchy, not merely on its existence.
2. Because survival at each stage is only \(p_i\), the overall reach probability \(P_{\mathrm{reach}}=\prod_{i=0}^{L-1}p_i\) can become tiny even when no single filter looks catastrophic in isolation.
3. Because filters such as triage, editorial selection, bureaucratic referral, algorithmic ranking, and expert validation each alter the \(p_i\)'s, salience is institutionally manufactured rather than naturally given.
4. Because public availability does not guarantee high \(P_{\mathrm{reach}}\), disciplined suspicion must audit attention pathways themselves and build counterweights for evidence that is probative but unlikely to survive the hierarchy.

## Objections this chapter must survive
- **Objection:** Salience often tracks genuine urgency, so distrusting what stands out risks flattening morally important differences. **Response:** The chapter argues for counterweights, not inversion; salient evidence must be checked against its rivals, not automatically discounted.
