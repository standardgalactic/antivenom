# Spine: ch081 — Constraint Relaxation

**Volume:** VII: Formal Synthesis
**Part:** XIV. Dynamics, Geometry, and Holonomy
**Status:** spine

## Claim
Constraint relaxation is governed by the procedural regularization parameter $\lambda(t)$: crisis lowers $\lambda(t)$, permitting larger belief-state updates from limited evidence and risking state-scale feature drift.

## Depends on
- ch080 (RSVP Fields of Public Interpretation) — shows how entrenched interpretive fields can lock inquiry into self-confirming paths
- ch076 (The Freedom Region) — provides the bounded space whose protections are put at risk when surveillance, inference, or intervention constraints are loosened

## Load-bearing steps
1. Let $\lambda(t)$ represent procedural regularization, the parameter that keeps ordinary revision constrained by evidentiary and procedural discipline.
2. Specify crisis or emergency power as a reduction in $\lambda(t)$, which permits larger updates on thinner evidence than would ordinarily be admissible.
3. Explain how lowering $\lambda(t)$ can be epistemically productive by opening trajectories that rigid procedures had blocked.
4. Explain how the same reduction threatens bounded reach, update discipline, and the freedom region by allowing rumor, panic, or opportunism to travel further than ordinary constraints would permit.
5. Frame the central question as whether a temporary fall in $\lambda(t)$ remains reversible or instead becomes durable feature drift at the scale of the state.

## Objections this chapter must survive
- **Objection:** Many suppressed truths emerge only because people violate existing constraints; insisting on safeguards may simply reinstall the very order that hid the truth.
- **Response:** The chapter permits genuine loosening but argues that corrective exceptions must be structured so their risks are not indefinitely offloaded onto the vulnerable.
