# Spine: ch095 — The Abuse of “More Context”

**Volume:** VIII: Applications and Case Structures
**Part:** XVI. Failure Modes of the Theory
**Status:** spine

## Claim
Context requests are legitimate only while added background could still materially alter the relevant threshold judgment; beyond that point, ``more context'' becomes a deferral tactic rather than an evidentiary need.

## Depends on
- ch094 (Epistemic Nihilism) — underdetermination is not indeterminacy, so the possibility of omitted context does not by itself suspend all judgment.
- ch073 (Evidentiary Reach) — context matters insofar as it changes how far a piece of evidence may responsibly travel.
- ch084 (Public Disorder and Mass Recording) — recorded fragments can mislead in isolation, but their partiality can also be exploited indefinitely to stall judgment.
- ch090 (Historical Reconstruction) — good contextualization reads documents within production conditions without making every claim hostage to infinite background.

## Load-bearing steps
1. Context is often essential because clips, quotations, incidents, and datasets can be seriously misread when severed from sequence, production conditions, or comparative scale.
2. The abuse begins when each supplied frame only generates a new request, without any account of what additional information could still change the conclusion that matters for the present decision.
3. This is where diminishing contextual returns matter: more surrounding material may still exist, but it no longer materially alters the warranted threshold judgment.
4. Institutions exploit the open-endedness of context demands because indefinite expansion can outlast public attention, witness availability, and remedy windows while preserving an appearance of fairness.
5. Responsible inquiry therefore needs a stopping rule keyed to material difference, not a fantasy of exhaustive background before any conclusion may be reached.

## External cases / technical analogues

| Case | Role | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Police-violence footage met with serial demands for earlier and wider framing | Load-bearing | Directly captures both sides of the problem: some context is necessary, but the demand can become indefinitely extensible. |
| Workplace complaints answered with endless requests for team-history and personality background | Illustrative | Shows context language functioning as HR delay rather than discrimination among live explanations. |

## Objections this chapter must survive
- **Objection / failure mode:** Without exhaustive context, institutions will punish people on the basis of misleading snippets and moral panics. **Response:** The chapter accepts the danger and answers it with a stopping rule keyed to whether the missing context could materially change the relevant decision.
