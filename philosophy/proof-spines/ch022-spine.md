# Spine: ch022 — Statutes, Reopening, and Finality

**Volume:** II: Causal Explosion
**Part:** IV. Time, Delay, and the Maturation of Evidence
**Status:** spine

## Claim
Finality should be set by the closure-time loss \(L(T)=L_{\mathrm{premature}}(T)+L_{\mathrm{delay}}(T)\), so reopening rules balance the costs of premature judgment against the costs of waiting.

## Depends on
- ch017 — responsible belief and proof are temporally structured.
- ch018 — immense observation time can create strong evidence only after long delay.
- ch019 — salience hierarchies can privilege the wrong signals early.
- ch020 — discovery curves vary by domain, so later evidence can rationally alter settled views.
- ch021 — initial narratives retain authority beyond their evidentiary warrant unless procedures preserve revisability.

## Load-bearing steps
1. Because closure time \(T\) carries both premature-error costs and delay costs, the relevant objective is the combined loss \(L(T)=L_{\mathrm{premature}}(T)+L_{\mathrm{delay}}(T)\).
2. Because \(L_{\mathrm{premature}}(T)\) captures errors frozen in before evidence matures, immediate finality is not neutral; it is one pole of a design trade-off.
3. Because \(L_{\mathrm{delay}}(T)\) captures the costs of suspended judgment, endless reopening is equally non-neutral and can destroy reliance, administrability, and the security of legal action.
4. Because the minimizing \(T\) varies with reversibility and severity, statutes, newly-discovered-evidence rules, clemency, and analogous devices should be designed as evidence-sensitive gates for structured corrigibility rather than as unconditional barriers or blank checks.

## Objections this chapter must survive
- **Objection:** Any meaningful reopening regime will be weaponized for endless relitigation, undermining deterrence, repose, and trust in judgments. **Response:** The chapter's answer is not unlimited reopening but narrow, evidence-sensitive, time-aware gates that distinguish corrigibility from perpetual instability.
