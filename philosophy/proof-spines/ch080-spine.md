# Spine: ch080 — RSVP Fields of Public Interpretation

**Volume:** VII: Formal Synthesis
**Part:** XIV. Dynamics, Geometry, and Holonomy
**Status:** spine

## Claim
Public interpretation is modeled by RSVP fields: a scalar field $\Phi$ of institutional potential, a vector field $\mathbf v$ of attention-and-evidence flow, and an entropy field $S$ of unresolved interpretive multiplicity.

## Depends on
- ch079 (Curvature of Reinterpretation) — establishes that reinterpretation bends nearby judgments unevenly and therefore invites analysis of what systematically channels that bending
- ch078 (Nonintegrability and Institutional Path Dependence) — shows why uptake cannot be summarized apart from the routes and ordering pressures through which publics receive evidence
- ch075 (Privacy–Publicity Duality) — contributes the account of public informational environments in which evidence is exposed, contested, and amplified

## Load-bearing steps
1. Introduce the scalar field $\Phi$ to represent institutional potential or concentrated authority across public space.
2. Introduce the vector field $\mathbf v$ to represent the directed transport of attention and evidence rather than treating publicity as neutral dissemination.
3. Introduce the entropy field $S$ to represent unresolved interpretive multiplicity, including zones where competing readings remain live or suppressed.
4. Use the joint variation of $\Phi$, $\mathbf v$, and $S$ to explain why identical evidence moves differently across differently configured publics.
5. Conclude that public interpretation is field-mediated: publicity transports evidence through an uneven environment instead of depositing it into a flat commons.

## Objections this chapter must survive
- **Objection:** Field language risks replacing individual responsibility with vague social atmospherics, making no one accountable for distorted interpretation.
- **Response:** Fields channel behavior without erasing agency; the point is to explain patterned pressure on judgment so responsibility can be assigned more accurately, not dissolved.
