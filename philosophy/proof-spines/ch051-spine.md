# Spine: ch051 — Continuation Geometry

**Volume:** V: Admissibility and Continuation
**Part:** IX. A General Theory of Institutional State Change
**Status:** spine

## Claim
\textbf{Continuation geometry} models persistence as transport \(T_\gamma:\mathcal J_x\to\mathcal J_y\), so a judgment remains the same only along authorized procedural paths.


## Depends on
- **ch047, "Record, Verify, Recognize, Authorize"** — continuity presupposes distinct stages whose outputs can be carried forward without confusion.
- **ch048, "Pop, Refuse, Bind, Collapse"** — continuation only makes sense against reversible, refusable, and binding transitions.
- **ch049, "Merge, Link, and Unlink"** — tracing identity through time requires distinguishing relation from fusion and preserving provenance.
- **ch050, "The Admissibility Boundary"** — only admissible states can be lawfully continued within a forum.

## Load-bearing steps
1. The chapter models persistence dynamically: a judgment space \(\mathcal J_x\) at state \(x\) is carried to \(\mathcal J_y\) along a procedural path \(\gamma\) by transport \(T_\gamma:\mathcal J_x\to\mathcal J_y\).
2. This replaces bare temporal sameness with path-dependent continuation: persistence, expiration, propagation, and interruption are all different transport outcomes.
3. Amendments, stays, remands, substitutions, and transfers matter because they alter \(\gamma\), not merely because they consume clock time.
4. The governing question is whether two different procedural paths transport a judgment to equivalent results or instead change what counts as the same case, status, or authorization.
5. Continuation geometry therefore supplies the formal bridge from admissibility to institutional identity across time.


## External cases / technical analogues

| Case | Role | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Amended pleading versus wholly new filing | Illustrative | Similar content can either preserve or sever case identity depending on the procedural path. |
| Appellate remand and agency handoff | Illustrative | Authorized transfer can preserve continuity while changing forum and timing. |

## Objections this chapter must survive
- **Objection:** Identity in institutions is conventional, so talk of geometry mystifies what is simply a declaration that a file or office remains the same. **Response:** Declarations work only when they map onto publicly traceable transitions that others can recognize as preserving authorized lineage.
