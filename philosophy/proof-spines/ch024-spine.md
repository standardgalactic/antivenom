# Spine: ch024 — The Evidentiary Haystack

**Volume:** III: Attention and Publicity
**Part:** V. Publicity Without Comprehension
**Status:** spine

## Claim
When \(N\) records contain only \(k\) relevant items, expected search cost scales like \(N/k\), so disclosure without indexing and provenance can manufacture opacity rather than eliminate it.

## Depends on
- ch023 — visibility without ordering and contestable standards of relevance does not itself produce knowledge.

## Load-bearing steps
1. The haystack begins with the search-cost fact that if \(N\) records contain only \(k\) relevant items, unguided inspection scales in expectation like \(N/k\).
2. Increasing \(N\) without comparable gains in indexing, preservation, or metadata therefore worsens retrieval even when formal access expands.
3. Ranking or summarization systems do not neutrally solve this, because their features encode an advance theory of relevance and thus pre-classify what can count as findable evidence.
4. This generates the paradox of search: theory-changing evidence is often hardest to locate precisely because existing theory determines the search keys.
5. Duties to disclose must therefore include preservation, indexing, summarization, and provenance so that unexpected evidence can become retrievable rather than merely present.

## External cases / technical analogues

| Case | Role | Note |
|---|---|---|
| FOIA-style document dumps released without usable metadata | Illustrative | Shows how nominal access can still defeat practical scrutiny. |
| The Panama Papers archive before journalistic indexing and collaboration | Illustrative | Demonstrates that disclosure became evidentiary only after organization. |

## Objections this chapter must survive
- Objection: modern search tools and AI summarizers largely solve haystack problems, so abundance is an inconvenience rather than a real barrier to knowledge.
- Response: search presupposes good labels, preserved context, and trustworthy provenance; when those are missing or strategically distorted, better tools shift but do not remove the asymmetry.
