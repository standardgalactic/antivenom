# Spine: ch099 — Bounded Transparency

**Volume:** IX: Institutional Design
**Part:** XVII. Designing for Delayed Truth
**Status:** spine

## Claim
Transparency should be bounded by purpose, audience, timing, and contestability through intermediate forms of disclosure rather than forced into secrecy or total publication.

## Depends on
- ch098 (Layered Verdicts) — public intelligibility requires institutions to disclose which layer of judgment they are announcing and on what reasons.
- ch029 (Privacy Beyond Secrecy) — privacy is a boundary condition for agency, not merely the hiding of embarrassing facts.
- ch061 (Strategic Transparency) — disclosure can be staged, selective, or weaponized, so visibility is not an unconditional remedy.
- ch075 (Privacy–Publicity Duality) — privacy and publicity co-produce reliable evidence and legitimate scrutiny when each limits the other's pathologies.

## Load-bearing steps
1. Because transparency serves accountability only in relation to a use case, disclosure must be designed around purpose, audience, timing, and contestability.
2. Because secrecy and universal publication are not the only options, institutions need intermediate forms such as delayed release, redaction, confidential review, selective disclosure, and independent escrow.
3. Because disclosure can itself injure privacy, safety, or fair process, the right question is who needs what information, when, and under what challenge conditions.
4. Therefore bounded transparency is a positive architecture of answerability, not a compromise between principle and convenience.

## External cases / technical analogues

| Case | Role | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Inspector-general or ombuds reports with redacted witness identities | Illustrative | Shows how reasons and findings can be public while vulnerable sources remain protected. |
| Classified-evidence summaries in adversarial proceedings | Illustrative | Illustrates the ambition to disclose enough for challenge without disclosing every operational detail. |

## Objections this chapter must survive
- **Objection:** Once officials are allowed to decide what the public needs to know, bounded transparency becomes a euphemism for self-serving secrecy.
- **Response:** The bounds must themselves be rule-governed, reviewable, and justified by publicly contestable purposes rather than left to discretionary preference.
