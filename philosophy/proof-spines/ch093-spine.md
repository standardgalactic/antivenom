# Spine: ch093 — When Publicity Is Necessary

**Volume:** VIII: Applications and Case Structures
**Part:** XVI. Failure Modes of the Theory
**Status:** spine

## Claim
Provisional publication is justified when delay would perpetuate harm and closed channels predictably bury the evidence, provided publication is treated as a reviewable warning or aggregation mechanism rather than as final adjudication.

## Depends on
- ch092 (When Privacy Conceals Domination) — some opaque domains block testimony aggregation so thoroughly that outside legibility becomes impossible without exposure.
- ch084 (Public Disorder and Mass Recording) — public evidence can widen accountability while still requiring redesigned standards for interpretation.
- ch085 (War, Intelligence, and Atrocity Evidence) — provisional public attribution may be necessary before courtroom-grade proof is available, but must remain matched to decision type.
- ch075 (Privacy–Publicity Duality) — publicity is one of the dual conditions by which evidence becomes contestable and power answerable.
- ch080 (RSVP Fields of Public Interpretation) — public exposure enters salience fields that can both reveal pattern and distort judgment.

## Load-bearing steps
1. Some harms continue while institutions wait, so the cost of silence is not neutral: delay can preserve exposure to danger, isolate victims, and destroy evidence.
2. In those settings, investigative reporting, registries, alerts, or open documentation may be warranted because private channels are predictably incapable of assembling a legible record.
3. The publication is still provisional in status even when necessary in practice; it should be framed as warning, pattern-making, or evidence aggregation rather than as the final moral or legal verdict.
4. This distinction matters because publicity can itself create new harms, so the case for disclosure depends on whether delay would do more damage than a bounded, reviewable release.
5. Publicity is necessary only when it supplies epistemic conditions for collective judgment that closed systems have systematically refused to create.

## External cases / technical analogues

| Case | Role | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Investigative publication about serial abuse after repeated private complaints fail | Load-bearing | Directly illustrates why disclosure can become necessary when delay itself protects ongoing harm. |
| Public-health or consumer-safety warnings issued before complete adjudication | Illustrative | A technical analogue for provisional publication that aims at protection rather than final blame. |
| Public misconduct registries or open atrocity archives | Illustrative | Shows publicity functioning as evidence aggregation rather than merely spectacle. |

## Objections this chapter must survive
- **Objection / failure mode:** Public exposure bypasses due process and can freeze provisional suspicion into irreversible social punishment. **Response:** Conceded in part; the chapter limits justified publicity to pattern-making and accountability functions when private systems are predictably nonlegible.
