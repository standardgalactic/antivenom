# Spine: ch008 — Recording as Selection

**Volume:** I: The Epistemology of Suspicion
**Part:** II. Events, Records, and Reconstructions
**Status:** spine

## Claim
Recording is constitutively selective because every record is generated as \(R=S_\theta(X)\), and \textbf{selection geometry} determines what the apparatus makes visible or invisible from the outset.

## Depends on
- ch006 — records are constructed traces rather than transparent copies of events.
- ch007 — provenance analysis shows that record-making is a chain of transformations rather than a single innocent transfer.

## Load-bearing steps
1. The chapter formalizes recording as \(R=S_\theta(X)\), making the selection regime \(\theta\) part of the object rather than an external afterthought.
2. It unpacks \(\theta\) into field of view, frame rate, spectral range, resolution, exposure, compression, retention, and metadata policy, showing that omission begins at capture.
3. The concept of \textbf{selection geometry} names the specific pattern of invisibilities created when a device is optimized for one evidential task rather than another.
4. That geometry explains why a system built to identify faces may fail to preserve gait, peripheral interaction, audio context, or environmental causation even when nothing is later altered.

## External cases / technical analogues

| Case | Role | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Body-camera or CCTV framing limits | Illustrative | Makes vivid that even continuous recording selects by angle, trigger, and retention rule. |
| Intake forms with fixed fields and drop-down categories | Illustrative | Shows how bureaucratic recording decides in advance what can be said or stored. |

## Objections this chapter must survive
- Many recording devices automatically capture whatever is in front of them, so calling recording essentially selective confuses later interpretation with the capture process itself.
- Response: automatic capture still presupposes selections of frame, trigger, sampling rate, category, and storage policy; neutrality disappears once those enabling choices are made visible.
