# Spine: ch069 — Algorithmic Accusation

**Volume:** VI: Adversaries, Platforms, and Automated Judgment
**Part:** XII. Platforms and Automated Publicity
**Status:** spine

## Claim
Algorithmic accusation must preserve the non-implication \(\operatorname{Flag}(x)\centernot\Rightarrow\operatorname{Violation}(x)\), since flags and anomaly scores mark suspicion rather than guilt.

## Depends on
- ch065 — platforms and related institutions are evidentiary systems whose records become inputs to later judgment.
- ch066 — moderation already functions as compressed adjudication, making automated triage and sanction part of an adjudicative process.
- ch067 — high-salience but unreliable material can feed automated suspicion and prioritization.
- ch068 — durable digital records harden into the raw material from which systems infer stable risk identities.

## Load-bearing steps
1. Define the system inputs named in the outline---risk scores and anomaly detectors---as mechanisms for generating suspicion at scale.
2. State the core non-implication \(\operatorname{Flag}(x)\centernot\Rightarrow\operatorname{Violation}(x)\), which separates triage from guilt.
3. Show that a system becomes accusatory when it collapses this distinction and treats statistical irregularity as though it already established wrongdoing.
4. Conclude that algorithmic pipelines may prioritize cases for review only if their procedures preserve the gap between flagging, investigation, and sanction.

## External cases / technical analogues

| Case | Role | Note |
|---|---|---|
| COMPAS-style recidivism scoring | Illustrative | canonical example of predictive inference functioning like accusation inside decision workflows |
| Platform trust-and-safety risk scores for account enforcement | Illustrative | shows accusation distributed across datasets, models, and queue rules rather than a single speaker |

## Objections this chapter must survive
- Statistical risk management is not accusation at all; large systems must triage with predictions, and importing accusatory norms will make governance impossible.
- Response: prediction is not condemned as such here; the claim is that once predictions steer adverse treatment, the protections appropriate to accusation become relevant.
