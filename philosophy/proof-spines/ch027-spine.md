# Spine: ch027 — Algorithmic Salience

**Volume:** III: Attention and Publicity
**Part:** V. Publicity Without Comprehension
**Status:** spine

## Claim
Algorithmic publicity is governed by the salience function \(\sigma(x\mid u,t,\theta)\), so what becomes visible is whatever an objective function ranks highly, not whatever is intrinsically probative.

## Depends on
- ch023 — visibility is not knowledge on its own.
- ch025 — control over attention allocation partly determines what a society can know.
- ch026 — inquiry can be disabled by engineered conditions that prevent stable focus.

## Load-bearing steps
1. Ranking systems assign prominence through the salience function \(\sigma(x\mid u,t,\theta)\), parameterized by item \(x\), user \(u\), time \(t\), and model state \(\theta\).
2. Visibility is therefore relative to an optimization target, so repeated exposure is a product of objective design rather than a transparent measure of importance.
3. The chapter distinguishes engagement, investigative, legal, scientific, and historical salience as different orderings over the same informational field.
4. Because those orderings diverge, the items that dominate a platform can be weak by the standards that matter for proof, science, or history.
5. Suspicion must therefore investigate the objective functions and feedback loops that manufacture \(\sigma\), not only the truth or falsity of the posts they elevate.

## External cases / technical analogues

| Case | Role | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Recommendation feeds on TikTok, YouTube, or comparable platforms | Illustrative | Makes feedback loops and repeat exposure legible as ranked outputs rather than neutral mirrors. |
| Trending-topic or “most engaged” lists | Illustrative | Shows how a metric can masquerade as a public verdict on relevance. |

## Objections this chapter must survive
- Objection: platforms largely reflect user preferences, so algorithmic salience is just the aggregate outcome of what people already wanted to see.
- Response: the proxies for “wanting” are themselves engineered, and feedback loops amplify prior exposure; the systems co-produce attention rather than neutrally report it.
