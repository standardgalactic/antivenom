# Spine: ch101 — Procedural Regularization

**Volume:** IX: Institutional Design
**Part:** XVII. Designing for Delayed Truth
**Status:** spine

## Claim
Procedures should be regularized in proportion to stakes and reversibility, with high-impact decisions receiving stronger review, slower updates, and rollback capacity.

## Depends on
- ch100 (Attention-Aware Archives) — evidence made usable still needs a disciplined sequence for uptake and response.
- ch035 (The Architecture of Proof) — coercive judgment needs a designed pathway from suspicion to consequence before it needs more confidence in perception.
- ch044 (Due Process as Constrained Updating) — procedure is valuable because it constrains update magnitude to tested evidentiary reach rather than vividness.
- ch057 (Bureaucracy and Slow Knowledge) — routine, queueing, and standardized review can be epistemic technologies rather than mere red tape.
- ch091 (When Procedure Protects Power) — regularization must be designed against the failure mode in which formally fair process launders hierarchy.

## Load-bearing steps
1. Because the adapter analogy shows why unconstrained updating can produce drift, institutional procedures should regularize revision before narrow evidence can reorganize high-stakes judgment.
2. Because error costs differ by domain, high-impact decisions need stronger regularization, independent review, slower update schedules, and explicit rollback mechanisms.
3. Because some choices are low-impact and reversible, those decisions can tolerate faster updates without inviting catastrophic lock-in.
4. Therefore procedural design should scale update friction to stakes and reversibility rather than treating every institutional pathway as equally permissive.

## Objections this chapter must survive
- **Objection:** Emergencies and novel harms rarely fit standardized scripts; regularization can freeze institutions into delay while agile adversaries exploit the gap.
- **Response:** The chapter must allow narrow, auditable exceptions while insisting that undefined exceptionality is exactly how discretion escapes epistemic discipline.
