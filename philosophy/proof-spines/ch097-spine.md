# Spine: ch097 — Reopenable Records

**Volume:** IX: Institutional Design
**Part:** XVII. Designing for Delayed Truth
**Status:** spine

**Foundational chapter** — opens a new Part with no upstream dependency. Prioritize reaching `reviewed` status here before drafting later chapters in this Part in earnest.

## Claim
Decision records must preserve provenance, uncertainty, dissent, dependency structure, and excluded alternatives so later evidence can reopen them responsibly.

## Depends on
- ch096 (The Abuse of Presumption) — presumptions are legitimate only when bounded by purpose, known error costs, and real avenues for reversal rather than allowed to harden into untouchable rule.
- ch022 (Statutes, Reopening, and Finality) — a just order must balance closure against the moral and epistemic necessity of reopening when evidence matures.
- ch017 (Evidentiary Time) — evidence has a temporal structure, so what can be responsibly believed depends on when in an inquiry one stands.
- ch056 (Archives and Institutional Memory) — archives determine whether institutions can remember enough of their own past to compare, revise, and answer for themselves.

## Load-bearing steps
1. Because delayed truth is normal, a decision record must preserve the provenance, uncertainty, dissent, and dependency structure of a conclusion rather than only its terminal label.
2. Because later review depends on seeing the road not taken, the record should also retain excluded alternatives and the reasons they were excluded at the time.
3. Because institutions still need workable closure, reopenability distinguishes practical closure from epistemic closure: action may occur now while the file remains explicitly revisable.
4. Therefore archives become corrigible when they store enough structure for later counterevidence to attach without pretending that the first settled entry was the last word.

## External cases / technical analogues

| Case | Role | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Post-conviction DNA exoneration files | Illustrative | Shows why preserving old evidentiary trails and decision rationales matters after a verdict has become administratively settled. |
| Medical-record amendment and audit histories | Illustrative | Demonstrates a live system that permits later correction without pretending the earlier entry never existed. |

## Objections this chapter must survive
- **Objection:** Reopenable records invite endless relitigation by making every settled decision look provisional, undermining reliance, deterrence, and administrative capacity.
- **Response:** Reopenability is keyed to preserved grounds and defined triggers for review, not to permanent wholesale rehearing.
