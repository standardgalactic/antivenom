\chapter{Continuation Geometry}
\label{ch:ch051}

A decision does not merely exist; it persists, expires, propagates, or is interrupted. \textbf{Continuation geometry} models that persistence as transport across institutional states:
\[
T_{\gamma}:\mathcal J_x\to\mathcal J_y,
\]
where \(\gamma\) is a procedural path and \(\mathcal J_x\) is the space of judgments available at state \(x\).

The central question is whether a judgment transported through different institutional paths remains equivalent. Amendments, stays, remands, transfers, substitutions, and delays do not simply consume time; they shape the path by which a judgment is carried. Continuation is thus geometric rather than merely chronological: sameness depends on the authorized route through the institutional field.
