\chapter{Distinction Holonomy}
\label{ch:ch052}

When a claim travels through police, media, court, archive, and public memory, it may return to its apparent starting point with altered meaning. If transport around a loop \(\gamma\) changes the claim,
\[
T_\gamma(J)\neq J,
\]
the system exhibits \textbf{distinction holonomy}.

This names a precise form of path dependence. Repeated representation can transform an accusation even when every individual transmission appears locally defensible. The chapter's claim is not merely that institutions reinterpret, but that lawful circulation itself can accumulate residue: a distinction may remain formally continuous while the journey changes what the distinction now does.
