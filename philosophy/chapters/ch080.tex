\chapter{RSVP Fields of Public Interpretation}
\label{ch:ch080}

This chapter cautiously adapts the RSVP vocabulary into a field description of public interpretation. A scalar field \(\Phi\) represents institutional potential or concentrated authority; a vector field \(\mathbf v\) represents the directed flow of attention and evidence; and an entropy field \(S\) represents unresolved interpretive multiplicity. These are not metaphysical substances, but formal devices for describing how publicity channels evidence through an uneven environment.

The governing intuition is that evidence does not enter a neutral public space. It is transported through varying concentrations of authority, directional flows of salience, and pockets of unresolved or suppressed multiplicity. The chapter therefore treats public interpretation as field-mediated rather than frictionless: what can be seen, amplified, stabilized, or contested depends on the shape of \(\Phi\), \(\mathbf v\), and \(S\) across the social space.
