\chapter{The Custodian Institution}
\label{ch:ch058}

The \textbf{custodian institution} does not dictate the final interpretation. It preserves records, boundaries, contestability, and routes of correction. Custodial authority is therefore distinct from sovereign authority.

This closing chapter reconnects Volume~V to the broader custodian architecture. An institution is genuinely custodial when it maintains the conditions under which later agents can reconstruct why a decision was possible, refused, or reversed. What it keeps intact is not only data, but the traceability of admissibility, continuation, and correction across time.
