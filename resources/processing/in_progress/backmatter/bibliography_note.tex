\chapter*{Bibliography Note}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Bibliography Note}

This monograph uses an inline bibliography rather than a separate
reference list, citing sources by name at the point of use. Seven prior
works from the same research program are engaged at three explicitly
distinguished tiers of confidence, set out in full in the front matter's
Relation to Prior Work and in Chapter~\ref{ch:four-operations}:
\emph{Conservation of Ambiguity}, Distinguishability Geometry,
Persistence Before Truth, and Observability Is Restorability are checked
against primary source text directly; \emph{Admissibility Before Truth}
and \emph{The Geometry of Correction} are integrated from formulas the
author transcribed from source PDFs, marked \transcribed{} throughout;
and \emph{The Geometry of Witnesses} is reported only from a prose
summary, marked \secondary{}. One further citation, to \emph{The
Geometry of Reconstruction}, was retracted during drafting; no such text
has been located, and Appendix~\ref{app:open-questions} records the
retraction rather than silently removing the trace of it.

External sources outside this research program are cited inline at
first mention rather than deferred here: the eyewitness-memory
literature referenced in Chapters~\ref{ch:mem8} and~\ref{ch:memory},
the oral-formulaic and stemmatic-philology literature referenced in
Appendix~\ref{app:extended-examples}, and Arrow's impossibility theorem
referenced in Chapter~\ref{ch:composition-failure} are each named at the
point they are used rather than collected into a separate list. This
monograph does not otherwise self-cite beyond the six works named above
and \emph{Conservation of Ambiguity}, from which the Redistribution
Theorem is imported in full in Chapter~\ref{ch:repair-economics}.
