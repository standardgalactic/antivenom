The symbol $\rho_W$ above is a renaming, introduced in this monograph,
of the reported $\rho(r)$ from The Geometry of Witnesses
(\S\ref{sec:witnesses}), undertaken specifically to avoid collision with
the unrelated coupling coefficient $\rho = \partial E/\partial O$ from
The Geometry of Correction, two sources that apparently chose the same
symbol independently (\cref{rem:rho-collision}).
